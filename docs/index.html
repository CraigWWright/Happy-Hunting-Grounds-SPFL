        The Rules

        • The aim of the game is to guess goalscorers for teams at away stadiums in the Scottish top division since 2004.
        • There are three rounds, where you will be given a team and a ground combination to guess.
        • You will first choose a difficulty level. 1 being the easiest, 10 being the hardest.
        • Answer with a player that you think has scored there and the season they scored.
        • If correct you will score points equal to the level you chose.
